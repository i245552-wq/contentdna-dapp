Prove you made it first.

Pick any file you made, and this page saves its fingerprint on the Sepolia testnet with your wallet and the time. The file never leaves your computer, only the fingerprint does.

How it works

  1. Connect MetaMask (make sure it's on Sepolia).
  2. Choose a file. Your browser makes its SHA-256 fingerprint.
  3. Give it a title and register it.
  4. Anyone can check the same file later and see who registered it.
